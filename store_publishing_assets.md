Since app stores require a URL link to a Privacy Policy, you can copy the markdown text below, host it on a free service (like GitHub Gist or GitHub Pages), and use that link:

```markdown
# Privacy Policy for Arbeitszeit Rechner

Last updated: June 2026

Arbeitszeit Rechner ("the App") is built as a local-only utility application.

### 1. Data Collection & Processing
The App does NOT collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, shift logs, locations, or usage statistics to any external servers or third parties. All data you input (such as work hours, name, hourly rate, and project logs) is stored strictly locally on your device using AsyncStorage.

### 2. Permissions
*   **Haptics:** Used solely to provide tactile vibrations when starting/stopping the timer.
*   **File Sharing/Storage:** Used solely to generate, write, and share PDF/CSV export files on your device.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party analytics, tracking libraries, or advertising networks.

### 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions, you can contact the developer through the official project repository.
```
